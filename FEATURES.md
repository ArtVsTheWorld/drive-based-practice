# Ace’s Expanded Practice 0.8.24

## Choose how to practice

Enter **Practice** from your offline Dynasty or from the game's main menu. Every session starts in **Default Practice**. Open **Pause > Mode** to cycle through three choices.

| Mode | What happens on the field |
| --- | --- |
| Default Practice | Pick both plays. The ball resets as usual so you can repeat the same rep. |
| Drive Practice | Call plays for your side. The CPU picks the other side's play. Carry the ball spot, down, distance and score from snap to snap. When the drive ends, try again from your starting spot. |
| Scrimmage | Call your side against the CPU and play through changes of possession. The teams can trade drives and build a score. |

Both drive modes show the yellow first-down line, keep score on the in-game scoreboard and return you to play selection. Your first snap counts. If both sides already chose plays when you changed modes, those calls can carry over. Use **Choose New Play** to pick again before that snap.

## Score a drive or a scrimmage

| What happened? | Drive Practice | Scrimmage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offensive touchdown | 6 points | 6 points |
| Interception or lost fumble | 4 defensive points if the defense keeps the ball without scoring a touchdown | The team that took the ball starts its drive where the return ends, with the usual touchback placement when needed. |
| Fourth-down stop | 2 defensive points | The other team gets the ball. |
| Defensive return touchdown | 6 points total | 6 points. Let the return play out. |
| Safety | The game can award 2 defensive points. | 2 points and the team that scored gets the ball. |

After a score in Scrimmage, the next drive starts at the 25. After an ordinary turnover or fourth-down stop, there are no bonus points in Scrimmage. These modes cover run and pass practice. There is no game clock, kickoff, extra point or kicking series.

## Set up another series

- **Re-spot Ball** gives you a fresh first down at the spot you pick. It is first and ten, or first and goal if you are closer than ten yards.
- **Flip Sides** starts a fresh drive with you controlling the other unit. Both this and Re-spot Ball keep your current score and stats.
- **Mode** clears the session's score and stats each time you change modes. Switch back to **Default Practice** for normal two-sided play selection.

## Check the stats

Open **Pause > Stats** in either drive mode. Each team has totals for practice plays, net offensive yards, first downs and offensive touchdowns. The defense also gets interceptions, fumble recoveries, stops on downs, return touchdowns, defensive points and safeties. Practice totals belong to this session.

This mod works on its own. It can share the supported Dynasty Extensions pregame controller screen. Other versions or changed controller screens need their own check. The module waits outside supported practice. Online games and ordinary matches use their normal controls. Defensive play selection does not show the opponent's offensive personnel.
