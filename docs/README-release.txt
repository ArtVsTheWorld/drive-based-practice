DRIVE-BASED PRACTICE 0.8.9 BETA
By Ace

Put SituationalPractice.dll in MMC Mod Manager's Plugins folder. Restart MMC
and launch the game through it. Enter practice in an offline Dynasty.

Default Practice is selected whenever you enter. It works like normal practice:
the ball resets to the same spot and you select plays for both sides. No setup
play is needed in any mode. Custom scoring, Stats and CPU play selection are off
in Default Practice.

Pause > Mode cycles Default Practice > Drive Practice > Scrimmage > Default Practice.
In the two drive modes, ball position, downs and distance advance. Pick your
side's play each snap; the CPU chooses the other side. Pause > Stats shows totals.
Your first snap counts toward the drive and stats; there is no throwaway setup play.
Drive Practice repeats drives from your chosen spot. Offensive TDs score 6;
defensive turnovers score 4, fourth-down stops 2, and return TDs 6 total.
Scrimmage changes possession after scores and turnovers. TDs score 6 and
safeties 2, with no turnover or stop bonus points.

In the drive modes, Flip Sides resets the drive and keeps scores/stats. Re-spot
Ball starts a new drive at the selected location. Changing modes clears scores
and stats. Returning to Default Practice restores the usual practice play picker.

No timed games, yellow first-down line, or kicking sequences in this beta.
Logs and crash reports are under Plugins/SituationalPractice.
The new mode transitions passed local checks; in-game testing is still needed.
